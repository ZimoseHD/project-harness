# Delivery

Deliver the task autonomously. Keep three things in view:

- **Issue:** Read the Issue to understand the task, desired outcome, and relevant context.
- **Reviewer:** Involve a reviewer to examine the work. Choose the reviewer, timing, and way of working to suit the task; [Review](review.md) offers guidance.
- **Context promotion:** Include [Context Promotion](context-promotion.md) in the work. Preserve useful knowledge for future tasks; when there is nothing worth recording, say so briefly.

Decide the approach, sequencing, decomposition, iterations, and completion actions yourself, using the task and the user's authorization. Other phase references are resources to draw on, not prescribed steps or a recovery state machine.
